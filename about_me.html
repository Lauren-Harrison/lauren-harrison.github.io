About Me

Who I Am

I am a 5th year Physics and Astronomy undergraduate student studying at the University of Victoria! I have goals after graduation to pursue my MSc degree in astronomy researching exoplanet populations and brown dwarfs.

I first found my love of physics in high school! I further found my love for astronomy after graduating high school and taking classes at Camosun College. I took two astronomy courses there and was instantly hooked! I knew astronomy was my calling, and I wouldn't be true to myself if I didn't pursue it.
