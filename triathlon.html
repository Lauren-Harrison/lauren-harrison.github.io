🏊‍♂️🚴‍♀️🏃‍♀️ Triathlon

I did my first triathlon in 2018! It was a youth triathlon and I was the last racer out of the water. I managed to work my up and finish 11th out of 16th in my age category. I really didn't care about the placement of my result, all I wanted was to complete my first triathlon! I remember being all smiles as I crossed the finish line :)

To this day, I've loved every moment of triathlon I've gotten to experience. I've gotten to fall in love with the sport over the years. There's something about not only the physical challenge, but also the mental challenge that calls to me. My ultimately goal, (one day), would be to complete an IRONMAN triathlon.
